1. Introduction

Welcome to WATCHON. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit our website, create an account, browse titles, or use available features.

Our goal is to provide a clear understanding of the information involved in using the platform and the choices available to users.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, WATCHON may handle the following types of information:

Account Information

Information such as your name, email address, and account details when registration is available.

Website Activity

Information about pages visited, titles browsed, saved items, and viewing progress where enabled.

Device Information

Basic technical information such as browser type, device type, and website performance information.

Contact Information

Information you provide when submitting questions or sending messages through contact features.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to support and improve the WATCHON website.

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Remembering items saved to My List.
  • Supporting viewing progress features.
  • Improving website performance and usability.
  • Responding to questions and support requests.
  • Protecting the website against misuse.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

WATCHON may use browser technologies such as cookies and local storage to support website features.

In the current browser-based version, a local profile, My List entries, and viewing progress may be stored directly in your browser.

Clearing browser data may remove information saved locally. A future version using online accounts may handle information differently.

Your Browser, Your Choice

You can manage or clear locally stored website data through your browser settings. Doing so may affect saved website features.

5. Sharing of Information

Personal information should only be shared when there is a valid reason, such as:

  • Providing a service requested by the user.
  • Maintaining website security.
  • Working with necessary service providers.
  • Meeting applicable legal requirements.

Any third-party service providers used by the production version of WATCHON should be identified in this policy.

6. Data Security

We recognise the importance of protecting information. Appropriate security measures should be used to reduce the risk of unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

Users should also protect their account information and avoid sharing passwords with other people.

However, no website or online system can guarantee complete security.

7. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on the services available and applicable privacy laws, users may have the right to:

  • Request access to their personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of eligible information.
  • Ask how their information is being used.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Users can contact the website operator to make a privacy-related request.

8. Children's Privacy

WATCHON should not knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental consent or other legal requirements apply.

Appropriate age-related protections should be implemented before public launch.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website features, services, or privacy requirements change.

Any updated version should be published on this page with its effective date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled, visit our Contact page.

Your Privacy Matters
