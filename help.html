Frequently asked questions

Find quick answers

Select Sign in from the navigation and enter your name and email. If you are new to WATCHON, this creates a simple profile in your browser.

Open Browse to explore the WATCHON collection. You can discover titles by category or use the search feature to look for a particular movie or series.

Open a title and use its My List button. Your saved titles can then be viewed from the My List page.

Check that your internet connection is working and refresh the page. If the issue continues, try again later or contact WATCHON for assistance.

Visit the Plans page to review the available WATCHON plan information.

Visit the Contact page and send your question to the WATCHON team.
